Good grading is mostly restraint. Start with these three moves.

## 1. Fix exposure and white balance first

Grading on top of a bad base only amplifies the problems.

## 2. Shape the contrast curve

A gentle S-curve adds depth. Lift the blacks slightly for a softer, filmic feel.

## 3. Split-tone with intent

Warm highlights and cool shadows is a classic pairing. Keep the saturation low.
